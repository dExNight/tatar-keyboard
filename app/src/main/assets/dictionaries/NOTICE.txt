Tatar top-100k dictionary data notice

This dictionary is derived from the Leipzig Corpora Collection downloadable
Tatar text corpora tat_mixed_2015_1M, tat_news_2015_1M, and tat_web_2018_1M.

Source catalogue: https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/en/download/tat
Terms: https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/en/usage

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Recommended citation:
D. Goldhahn, T. Eckart, U. Quasthoff, "Building Large Monolingual
Dictionaries at the Leipzig Corpora Collection: From 100 to 200 Languages",
LREC 2012.

Changes by the Tatar Keyboard project: the three word-frequency lists were
decoded, validated, normalized to NFC lowercase, filtered to the modern Tatar
Cyrillic alphabet and a 64-code-point limit, merged with summed frequencies,
ranked, cut to 100,000 forms, lexically reordered, packed into a versioned
binary format with SHA-256, and zlib-compressed. The dictionary data remains a
separate CC BY 4.0 component; the application's Apache-2.0 license does not
replace that data license.
